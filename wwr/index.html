Ready for an adventure? Check out our trips

Purpose

To provide thrilling and safe white water rafting adventures for individuals, families, and groups looking to experience the excitement of nature, build camaraderie, and create unforgettable memories on the river.

Offerings

Guided White Water Rafting Tours: Half-day, full-day, and multi-day rafting trips led by certified guides on Class II–V rapids, suitable for all experience levels.

Custom Group Packages: Tailored experiences for corporate team-building, school outings, bachelor/bachelorette parties, and family reunions, including meals, transportation, and optional camping.

Beginner & Safety Clinics: Hands-on training sessions to teach paddling techniques, river safety, and rescue skills—perfect for first-timers or those looking to improve their confidence on the water.
